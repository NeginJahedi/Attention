# Analysis

## Layer 2, Head 5

Each token is paying attention to its previouse token.

Example Sentences:
- He ate the [MASK] apple slowly. 
    [MASk] : adj e.g red
- She read the dusty book [Mask].  
    [Mask] : adv e.g quietly 


## Layer 3, Head 11

Verbs are paying attention to their direct objects.
    In the 1st sentence "ate" is paying attention to "apple" (apple is what being eaten)
    In the 2nd sentence "read" is paying attention to "book" (book is what being read.)

Example Sentences:
- He ate the [MASK] apple slowly. 
    [MASk] : adj e.g red
- She read the dusty book [Mask].  
    [Mask] : adv e.g quietly
